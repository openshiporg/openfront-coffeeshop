## Major

### QA-201: The backfill published the admin's login email as the cafe's public contact
- **Area:** storefront / onboarding
- **URL:** `/` (Hours & location → Contact), `/dashboard/platform/store-settings`
- **Steps:** Open the storefront home page and scroll to Contact.
- **Expected:** The public contact is an address the merchant chose to publish. If the old seed value has to go, it's cleared and the merchant is prompted to add one (the field is required in Store Settings).
- **Actual:**
  - The page shows `qa+coffee@openship.org`, the admin's sign-in email.
  - The round-2 migration writes the earliest settings-capable staff user's email into `StoreSetting.contactEmail` with no prompt.
  - On a real store, that's the owner's personal login address, published on the public site and given to crawlers without their consent.
- **Likely files:**
  - `migrations/20261023200000_coffee_seed_backfill/migration.sql` (the `contactEmail` update).
  - A follow-up should clear a value that equals a staff login email and flag Store Settings as incomplete, rather than publish it.
  - `features/storefront/screens/HomePage.tsx` could also hide the contact email when it matches a user's email.

### QA-202: KDS shows tomorrow's order-ahead tickets tonight, ahead of the live counter order, with a timer running
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URL:** `/dashboard/platform/kds`
- **Steps:**
  1. At 8:30 PM Friday, with the store closed, accept the two order-ahead requests for Saturday 8:15 AM.
  2. Ring up a counter croissant on the POS.
  3. Open the KDS.
- **Expected:**
  - Scheduled pickups stay out of the make queue, or sit in a separate "Later / scheduled" group, until close to their pickup time (for example promisedAt minus prep time).
  - The make queue is ordered by when items are due.
  - The wait timer counts toward the pickup time, not from acceptance.
- **Actual:**
  - "5 to make". The Pastry Case column lists #CF-261023-16E4CEDA (107m) and #CF-261024-11FF46E8 (5m), both "Tomorrow 8:15 AM EDT", **above** tonight's counter order #CF-261024-DE03B844 (2m, 8:45 PM).
  - At opening, those tickets will show about 12 hours of wait.
  - A barista working top-down would make tomorrow's croissant first.
  - #CF-261024-11FF46E8 shows its lines as "Queued" on the KDS while the order is only "Accepted": "Send to queue" was never pressed. Acceptance already queues the lines, so the Orders "Send to queue" step doesn't control what the bar sees.
- **Likely files:**
  - `features/keystone/mutations/coffeeBoundaryQueries.ts:339-355` (`getCoffeeKdsBoard`): it orders by `queuedAt` only, and doesn't filter on `promisedAt` or order status beyond `KDS_ACTIVE_ORDER_STATUSES`.
  - `features/platform/kds/components/KdsBoard.tsx:167` (`waited` from `queuedAt`).
  - `features/keystone/mutations/acceptCafePickupOrder.ts`: line status on accept.
